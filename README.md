Smart Accident Analytics leverages modern data science techniques to analyze and interpret traffic accident data, providing a clearer understanding of road safety trends. By using real-time data from sources such as GPS, traffic cameras, sensors, and historical accident reports, this approach helps identify high-risk areas, common causes of accidents, and patterns over time. The goal is to support better decision-making for traffic management, urban planning, and public safety initiatives, ultimately reducing the number and severity of road accidents.
